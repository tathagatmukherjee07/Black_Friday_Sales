# Black Friday Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
End-to-end Black Friday Sales Analysis project focused on uncovering customer purchasing patterns, product performance, demographic trends, and city-level sales insights.

## Business Problem
The project analyzes Black Friday retail transactions to understand customer behavior, product demand, demographic differences, and geographic sales patterns, supporting better marketing, loyalty, cross-selling, and inventory decisions.

## Objectives
- Analyze customer demographics and purchasing behavior
- Identify high-value customer segments
- Evaluate product category performance
- Analyze city-level sales contributions
- Perform statistical hypothesis testing
- Build interactive Power BI dashboards
- Develop actionable business recommendations

## Dataset
- **Transactions:** 550,068
- **Columns:** 12
- **Data includes:** customer demographics, city information, product categories, and purchase amounts
